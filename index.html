About Valtriva

Time Before Chops

Founded by touring session drummer Marlow Deshane, Valtriva was built on a simple belief: flashy fills mean nothing without rock-solid time. Every student builds real groove and internal timing before chasing speed or complexity.

  • ✓ Instructors with touring and session careers
  • ✓ Groove and timing built into every single lesson
  • ✓ Programs for total beginners through gigging drummers
  • ✓ Real play-along tracks, not just isolated exercises
